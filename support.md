# Sartelle Support

Your wardrobe, styled by someone who has seen all of it.

## Contact us

Email **[mustafa@mustafacerit.com](mailto:mustafa@mustafacerit.com)**. We answer
within two business days. Write in whichever language you prefer — if we cannot
read it, we will translate it rather than ask you to switch. If you are writing
about something the app generated, please include the date and what you were
doing, so we can find the exact result.

You can also report a generated result from inside the app: press and hold a
stylist reply, or tap the flag next to the AI badge under a try-on render.
Reports reach the same inbox.

## Common questions

### Do I have to let Sartelle analyse my photos?

No. Analysis is opt-in and you are asked before any photo leaves your device.
If you decline, you can still add garments by hand — including their photos —
and the wardrobe, outfit builder, planner and trip packing all work normally.
You can withdraw the permission at any time from Profile → Privacy & data.

### Where are my photos processed?

By Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service on Sartelle's behalf, in the European Union
(Sweden). That is where processing happens for everyone, wherever you are — we
do not route photos to a different region depending on your country. Your
photos are never used to train AI models and are never sold. See the
[Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for the full list of processors and retention
periods.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by Sartelle. Open the Settings app → tap
your name → Subscriptions, or use
[apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).
Cancelling at least 24 hours before the period ends stops the next renewal.

### I bought a plan but the app still shows the free limits.

Open Profile → Plan and tap **Restore purchases**. That re-synchronises your
entitlement with the App Store. If it still does not appear, email us with the
date of purchase and we will fix it.

### What are credits, and why did one get used?

Credits pay for the work an AI request costs:

| Action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| AI styling suggestion | 1 |
| Catalog cutout | 2 |
| Garment analysis (per batch) | 3 |
| Try-on render (per pose) | 6 |

Plans include a monthly allowance; credit packs top it up. A request that fails
does not consume credits.

### The try-on does not look like me.

Try replacing your reference photos with a well-lit, front-facing portrait and a
full-body shot against a plain background. If it is still wrong, report it from
the render — that flag exists for exactly this.

### How do I export or delete my data?

Profile → Privacy & data. **Request data export** builds a downloadable archive
of everything on your account. **Delete my account** is immediate and permanent:
it removes your account, your sign-in identity, every photo and all data.
Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that
separately in the App Store.

### Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all purchases and refunds for Sartelle, so refund requests go to
Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Whatever
rights your country gives you over a digital purchase apply there; we cannot
issue a refund on Apple's behalf. If the reason is something the app got wrong,
write to us as well — we would rather fix it.

### What languages does Sartelle speak?

Twenty: Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English,
Finnish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Polish,
Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish and Ukrainian. The app
follows your device language on first launch and you can change it any time from
Profile → Language. The stylist, garment analysis and notifications all answer in
the language you picked, not the one your device happens to be set to.

### Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
